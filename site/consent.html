SMS Consent and Opt-In Policy
Unassigned is an anonymous link-sharing service operated in the United States. This page explains exactly how recipients consent to receive text messages, in accordance with carrier and TCPA requirements. No marketing or promotional messages are ever sent. All messages are transactional and require the recipient's explicit opt-in before any content is delivered. This page and the service itself are open to anyone: no password, no login and no account is required.

How recipients opt in

Consent is collected via text (SMS reply). A recipient is never sent link content until they have explicitly replied to confirm. The exact sequence:

  1. A sender enters a recipient's phone number and a link on the Unassigned website.
  2. The recipient receives a single one-time consent request. This first message contains no link, only the opt-in request.
  3. The recipient replies Y to consent. Only then is the link delivered.
  4. If the recipient does not reply, nothing further is sent. If they reply STOP, they are opted out permanently and any held content is deleted.

The opt-in message recipients receive

Hi, this is Unassigned. You have a link from ASSIGNED123ABC. Reply Y to receive it. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg and data rates may apply.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies and depends on how often someone shares a link with the recipient. Recipients only ever receive messages tied to a specific link shared with them. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time or HELP for help.

Privacy

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver the consent request and, after opt-in, the shared link. Numbers are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing. A recipient who opts out has their number recorded only to honor that opt-out, and any held content is deleted.
